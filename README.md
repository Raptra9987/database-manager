## 📖 How to Use database-manager

### Step 1: Launch the Application
Double-click the database-manager icon on your desktop or in your Start menu.

### Step 2: Add Your Files
Click **"Add Files"** and select one or multiple Excel, CSV, TXT, or vCard files from your computer. You can add files one by one or select them all at once.

### Step 3: Choose Your Data Type
Select whether your data contains **contacts**, **products**, **students**, or **custom data**. The app will automatically adjust its columns accordingly.

### Step 4: Merge and Review
Click **"Merge"** to combine all your files. Review the results in the preview window. Look for duplicate highlights (marked in yellow) and verify the source tracking column.

### Step 5: Save Your Database
Click **"Save"** to export your merged database as a single Excel file. Choose a location on your computer and give your file a name.

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

- **Clean your files first:** Remove unnecessary headers or repeated data before merging
- **Use consistent formatting:** Ensure dates, numbers, and text are formatted the same way across files
- **Review duplicates carefully:** Some duplicates may be intentional (e.g., customers with multiple orders)
- **Save backups:** Always keep original files until you're satisfied with the merged result

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to install anything else?
No. database-manager is a standalone application. It runs on its own without additional programs.

### Can I undo a merge?
Yes. Most actions have an undo button. If you accidentally merge wrong files, simply click **Undo**.

### Will my original files be modified?
Never. database-manager only reads your files. Your originals remain untouched.

### Can I merge more than 100 files?
Absolutely. There is no file limit. The application handles small batches and large collections equally well.

### How do I know which file a row came from?
Look for the **"Source File"** column in your merged database. It displays the filename for every row.

### Does it work with Excel 2007?
The application works with modern Excel formats (.xlsx). For older formats (.xls), convert your files first using Excel's **"Save As"** function.
